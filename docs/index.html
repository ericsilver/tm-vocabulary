Vocabulary position in the US trademark record

Every US trademark application describes the goods or services the mark will cover, and every registered mark's owner must later swear that the product is still being sold. This project re-parses all 13.99 million USPTO trademark case files into dated events, and scores each goods/services description on two axes: atypicality, how unusual its language is for its industry, and lead, whether its language ran ahead of or behind where the industry's language was moving. Both compare the description's mix of fifty themes with its own Nice class's filings in the five years before and the five years after it was filed.

Papers

  • An Event-Dated Corpus of US Trademark Prosecution and a Two-Sided Measure of Vocabulary Position — the corpus, the measure, and its validation.
  • Arrows in Their Backs: Vocabulary Lead and Product Survival in the US Trademark Record — whether being early pays: product survival, funding, and listing.
  • Business Themes in the Trademark Record (SSRN 6954598) — the earlier combined working paper.
