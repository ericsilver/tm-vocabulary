\section*{Where being leading costs: the factors together}

\emph{For the average registration, leading costs 1.1 points of
survival once the factors are held; without them, 2.3.} About half of
the raw penalty comes from who files leading language and what it
describes: leading filings are more often self-filed, more often
platforms, and more often use the vocabulary of general-purpose
technologies, and all three survive less.

\emph{Counsel is the largest moderator.} Counsel-represented
registrations survive the proof 18.7 points more often, and among them
leading costs nothing (+0.1 points); among self-filed registrations the
most leading filing survives 5.1 points less often than the most
lagging. Counsel is the first term to enter the LASSO.

\emph{Patenting marks a larger, not a more durable, portfolio.} Owners
who patent survive the proof 1.7 points less often, as do owners with
25 or more earlier filings (10.3 points less often): large portfolios
let more marks lapse. Conditional on patenting, leading neither helps
nor hurts detectably ($-0.9$ points, not significant); the interaction
is $+0.2$.

\emph{Platforms and general-purpose-technology vocabulary survive less
and pay more for leading.} Platform and network offerings survive 5.6
points less often, and among them leading costs 6.2 points. Filings
using internet, mobile, cloud, social-media, AI or blockchain vocabulary
survive 5.1 points less often, and leading costs them 3.0 points.
Emergent consumer categories survive 12.6 points less often and
invention vocabularies 6.6, with no detectable change in the lead
effect.

\emph{Leading costs more where a class's filing volume is growing.}
Each SD of five-year growth in the class's filing volume adds 1.0 point
to the penalty; at one SD above the mean, leading costs 2.1 points.
Filings in boom years pay 1.8 points less than other years once volume
growth is held, the opposite of the prediction.

\emph{Geography moves survival, not the lead penalty.} Each SD of a
theme's co-location lowers survival by 1.6 points and each SD of growth
from first-time filers by 1.4; being in a hub or in a theme with a hub
changes neither survival nor the penalty detectably.

\emph{Classes fall into three groups, and the grouping holds out of
sample.} Grouped on half of owners and estimated on the other half,
leading costs 5.5 points in tobacco, yarns, wine and spirits, technology
services, medical devices, fuels, lighting and heating, meat and dairy,
carpets, chemicals, metals and machinery; about nothing in the middle
group; and it is rewarded, by 7.1 points, in paints, legal and personal
services, lace and ribbons, and beer and soft drinks. After counsel, the
two class groups are the next terms to enter the LASSO, ahead of
filing-volume growth, platforms and technology vocabulary.
