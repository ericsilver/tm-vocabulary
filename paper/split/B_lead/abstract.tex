\begin{abstract}
\noindent Are pioneers the ones with arrows in their backs? The US
trademark record can answer at census scale: a trademark application is
a dated description of a product a firm expected to sell, and the record
later states, under oath, whether the product was still in commerce five
years on. Each of 13.99 million applications is scored on how far its
goods/services language sat toward where its industry's language was
moving (its lead) and on how unusual it was for its industry (its
atypicality), and each mark and its owner is followed through
registration, the five-year proof of continued use, the year-ten renewal,
a priced private round, SEC reporting and listing. Being early costs.
Among 3.10 million registrations, the most leading fifth fail the
five-year proof $1.4$ points more often than the most lagging fifth,
within Nice class and registration year with drafting and counsel held
($t = 8.3$), in three of every four classes and under every partition of
the vocabulary tried. The penalty was two to three times larger for
late-1990s filings, reversed for 2000--2004 filings, and returned from
2008. The swing sits inside four technology classes and is mostly
between themes, each era's leading vocabulary concentrated in one or two
themes that failed at their own rate --- and the reversal itself
decomposes: the dot-com services vocabulary, by then lagging, failed at
the era's worst rates and flips the pooled sign, while a genuine reward
for leading concentrated where internet language met goods, strongest in
e-commerce retail. Being
unusual does not cost at the proof: its apparent protection belongs to
filings extending a theme from another class. The two evaluators the
record supplies read the text differently: the examiner at registration
rewards middling conformity and cannot observe lead, and the market
penalizes having been early. No later rung rewards it either. The debut's
language does not predict a priced round, SEC reporting and listing fall
in lead, and the firms holding most of the linked revenue debuted in
language their industries already used.

\medskip
\noindent\emph{Keywords:} pioneering; first-mover disadvantage; optimal
distinctiveness; trademarks; product survival; text as data.\\

\end{abstract}
