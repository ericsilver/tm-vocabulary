\section{Access}\label{sec:access}

The code is public at \url{https://github.com/ericsilver/tm-vocabulary}:
the parser from the USPTO case files to dated events, the theme model's
fitting and scoring, the owner links, every analysis script, and every
generated figure and table in this paper. The derived tables are
published as a versioned data release of the same repository
(\url{https://github.com/ericsilver/tm-vocabulary/releases/tag/data-v1}):
the 242 million dated prosecution events and the counsel, filing-basis
and declaration fields read alongside them; an event-dated outcome table
giving, for every registered mark, the date and registration age of any
cancellation at the five-year proof of continued use; the fitted theme
model and its vocabulary; the per-filing scores under the production
scoring; and the owner links to SEC registrants and Form~D issuers.
Every table rebuilds from the public USPTO, SEC and PatentsView sources
with the scripts provided.

The data can be browsed and explored at
\url{https://aporia.institute/tm-vocabulary/}. Its online appendix,
\url{https://aporia.institute/tm-vocabulary/online-appendix/}, lists every
theme with a label, its leading words and its share of each Nice class,
gives per-class breakouts of the five-year-proof and registration
results, and includes a viewer of each class's registrations on the
lead/atypicality plane. Researchers who want the five-year proof as an
outcome without the text measure can take the event-dated outcome table
directly; those who want the measure on a different corpus can refit the
theme model with the scripts provided.
