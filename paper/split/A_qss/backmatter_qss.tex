\section*{Data availability}
The derived data --- the dated prosecution events, the event-dated
outcomes, the theme model, and the per-filing scores --- are deposited
at Zenodo \citep{SilverData2026}. All code and every generated figure
and table are public at \url{https://github.com/ericsilver/tm-vocabulary},
and an interactive per-class online appendix is at
\url{https://aporia.institute/tm-vocabulary/online-appendix/}. The source
data are public: the USPTO trademark backfile \citep{USPTOtrtyrap}, the
SEC's financial statement data sets, company ticker file and Form~D data
sets \citep{SECfsds, SECtickers, SECformd}, and PatentsView
\citep{PatentsView}.
